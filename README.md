# Stackfive

Our COS 209 group repo for the semester. Everything we submit for the course lives here.

## What this is

Our first task is a Product Requirements Document (PRD) built around a real problem. We picked repairs in the university halls of residence, and we are looking at why they take so long and why students can't see where their request stands. We are collecting the evidence ourselves through interviews and a student survey, and the solution in the PRD will be based on what we find.
